# DESIGN_BIBLE — Wildspark Kart

## 1. Vision
An original arcade kart racer for phones where the fun comes from **mastering tracks**: power-slides that feed a boost reserve, lines that trade distance for momentum, shortcuts that unlock in layers, and lush, living, stylized worlds. Inspired by the *design philosophy* of classic mascot kart racers (deep slide/boost loops, layered shortcuts, dense environments) — **no** names, characters, tracks, art, audio or UI from any existing game are used. All content is generated by this project's own code (meshes, textures, audio).

Pillars (in priority order): 1) handling feel, 2) one deep interconnected track, 3) rich environment, 4) competition & UI, 5) performance, 6) more content.

## 2. Handling model (scripts/kart/kart.gd, kart_params.gd)
- **Heading ≠ velocity.** The chassis yaw and the travel direction are separate; lateral grip pulls velocity toward the heading. Absorbed lateral slip is converted back to forward speed minus a *scrub* (0.16 steering, 0.07 sliding) → momentum is conserved through corners.
- **Acceleration:** 17 m/s² at standstill tapering with `(v/cap)^1.7`; base top speed 26 m/s (balanced). 0→90 % in ~3 s (target, see tests).
- **Steering:** full authority from 9 m/s; yaw 2.35 rad/s at 9 m/s → **1.05 rad/s at top speed (R ≈ 26 m)**. Tight corners at speed require sliding — by design.
- **Power slide:** hop (4.6 m/s) + steer → slide on landing. Yaw = 1.45 ± 0.95 rad/s (steer into/out of the slide) → R ≈ 11 m at full speed with full lock. Drift grip 2.6/s → steady slip angle ≈ 25–35°.
- **Spark meter:** 3 stages per slide, fill times 0.95/0.82/0.72 s. Tap BOOST in the hot zone (≥ 70 %); ≥ 90 % = PERFECT. Early tap = fizzle (meter resets, stage kept, time lost). Waiting past 106 % = backfire (stage lost). Perfect rewards: +0.55/+0.75/+1.0 s reserve and +1.4/+1.9/+2.5 m/s kick; good taps scale 0.55–0.85.
- **Boost reserve:** seconds of boost stored (cap 4.5 s), burned at 1×/s × surface drain (grass 1.8, sand 2.2, mud 3.0). While reserve > 0: tier 1 **Boost** (+24 % cap) or tier 2 **Overdrive** (+40 % cap, burns 1.15×). Overdrive comes from Overdrive pads, a charged Spark Canister, or chaining the reserve above 3.6 s. When the reserve empties, excess speed bleeds at 4.5 m/s² (momentum, not a cliff).
- **Sources:** sparks, pads (top up to 2.2 s / 3.4 s), air boosts (hang time > 0.5 s, clean landing: +0.9 s per s above 0.38 s, max 1.2 s), start boost (+1.3 s), Spark Canister item.
- **Hook turn** (advanced U-turn): while sliding, pull back (stick down / brake) with steer into the slide → yaw 3.3 rad/s, bleeding 9 m/s². A spark within 0.4 s after releasing = **Hook Boost** (+45 %). Optional, repeatable, keeps momentum when done right.
- **Air:** turn 1.25 rad/s, velocity nudged ≤ 0.22 rad/s toward the heading; bad landings (> 30° slip) cost 18 % speed; heavy landings 4 %.
- **Impacts:** sin(impact angle) < 0.26 graze (−3 %, −0.15 s reserve), < 0.62 glance (−14…−40 %, reserve ×0.4, chassis deflected along the wall), else head-on (−75 %, reserve lost, 0.35 s stun, bounce). Contacts near the capsule's bottom are bumps, not walls.
- **Surfaces** (scripts/kart/surfaces.gd): grip, slide grip, top-speed, accel, drag, drain, charge-rate and flow multipliers; each surface is a strategic trade-off, not decoration.
- **Determinism:** fixed 60 Hz physics, no randomness in kart physics.

### Handling targets (asserted by tests/physics_tests.gd — 33/33 pass in Godot 4.7.2, 2026-11-23)
top speed ±0.6 m/s · 0→90 % in 2.0–4.5 s · steering R at top speed 20–32 m · slide slip 15–45° · slide R < 16 m · 3 perfect sparks ≈ 2.3 s reserve · early tap fizzles / late backfires · reserve drains 1 s/s · Overdrive ≈ +40 % · chained slides sustain boost · hop 0.25–0.5 s · ramp air boost · graze/glance/head-on classification · hook > 1.3× slide yaw · current exceeds road cap · determinism.

## 3. Track design rules
1. Design driving first (radii, widths, heights, surfaces), dress the world second.
2. Every zone has an identity, a skill test, and a reason to arrive with a particular speed/reserve/angle (see TRACK_DESIGN §2 "chains").
3. Five mastery layers: learn the route → slide timing → better lines → linking zones → frame-perfect execution (lip-hops, hook boosts, keeping Overdrive).
4. Shortcuts come in types: distance, momentum, precision jump, hidden, interaction, high-risk, multi-stage. None is free; each has a failure state that costs time but never a hard reset.
5. Vary width (9–18 m), radius (15–150 m), banking (−9…+9°) and elevation deliberately; avoid repetition.
6. Obstacles must be visible ≥ 2 s ahead at top speed, have a rhythm or a gap, and test one thing.
7. Readability: chevron boards on corner outsides, landmarks per zone, clear surface colours.

## 4. Visual rules
- **Stylized high-fidelity:** smooth, chunky shapes with lots of secondary detail; vertex-painted occlusion and colour variation; triplanar textured rock/bark/stone with moss on upward faces; anti-tiled painted grass; flowing water with foam; wind on foliage.
- **Three layers:** near (roots, rocks, grass, flowers, signage), mid (forest mass, set pieces, terrain), far (rising hills, mountain rings, Skyveil Falls, watchtower).
- Every zone gets a landmark. The world continues past the road (terrain extends 170 m beyond the track bounds, then mountains).
- No empty spaces unless they're intentional vistas (river, meadows).
- Colour: warm sun (1.0, 0.95, 0.84), saturated greens, teal water, orange boosts / teal Overdrive.
- **Performance strategy (keep richness, render smart):** MultiMesh cells with visibility ranges, two LODs for trees/rocks, distance-faded grass, chunked terrain with LOD + skirts, shared materials, shadows only near (≤ 95 m), quality presets that scale density (×1.0/1.3/1.7 spacing) rather than removing whole layers.

## 5. Controls (touch first)
Left: floating stick (steer; pull back = brake / hook while sliding). Right: big DRIFT (hold); re-tapping DRIFT within 0.16 s while sliding = spark BOOST (one-thumb slide + boost); separate BOOST for claw grips; ITEM, BRAKE, BACK; ACCEL appears only if auto-accelerate is off. Size, opacity, positions editable; safe-area aware; haptics on sparks/impacts. Keyboard & gamepad mapped too.

## 6. AI
Lane graph over racing lines (minimum-curvature, obstacle-aware) with braking-aware speed profiles. Per-difficulty: slide usage, tap accuracy (perfect/hot/early/never), line noise, steering noise, corner margin, shortcut appetite (conditioned on actual kart speed/tier), lip-hops & hook turns (Hard+), item IQ. Mistakes emerge from these parameters, not scripted crashes.
**Rubber banding policy:** "Light" by default — only AI far behind the *player* (> 90 m) gets up to +1.8 % top speed (max +3 % on "Classic"); AI ahead never gets slowed artificially. Can be turned off.

## 7. Items
Bramble Bomb (arc, bounce, 4.2 m blast), Homing Hornet (follows the track to the kart ahead), Thorn Trap (dropped), Bubble Shield (10 s / until hit), Spark Canister (+2 s reserve). Position-weighted drops. 10 amber = **charged** items (bigger blast, faster hornet, Overdrive canister, shield until hit). Projectiles break the Woodcutter's Gate.

## 8. Audio
All synthesized by tools/gen_audio.py: engine loop (pitch follows speed/tier), stage-pitched spark chimes, perfect chord, fizzle/backfire, pads, impacts, splash, UI, countdown, original 138 BPM race loop.

## 9. Originality checklist
Game name, characters (Rusk, Bandit, Ribbo, Bramble, Hoots, Clover, Tusker, Splash), items, track, terms (Spark, Reserve, Boost/Overdrive, Hook turn) are original. No external assets.
